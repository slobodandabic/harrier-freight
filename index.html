    FAQ

    Questions
    we get a lot.

    Don't see yours? Dispatch picks up 24/7 at (773) 555-0142.

    Are you a broker or a carrier?

    An asset-based carrier. We own the trucks and trailers and employ the drivers. We never re-broker your load.

    How fast can you pick up?

    Inside the Chicago metro, usually same day if you book before 11 a.m. The rest of the Midwest is next day. Expedited team pickups have a 2-hour window.

    How do I track a shipment?

    Every load gets a tracking link that updates from the truck's ELD every 15 minutes, plus texts at pickup, at any delay and at delivery, with a POD photo.

    Do you haul hazmat?

    Most classes, with hazmat-endorsed drivers. Put the class and UN number in the quote form and we confirm.

    What are your payment terms?

    Net 30 for approved shippers. New customers can book first loads by card or quick-pay while credit is set up, usually within 24 hours.

    I'm a driver with less than a year. Do you hire?

    Local and dedicated runs take 6+ months of verifiable CDL-A experience and a clean MVR. Regional and OTR need one year.
